Vision

A people rooted in Christ, alive in service

We aspire to be a God-loving, life-giving, and responsible Christian community — disciples of Christ and a Church of the poor, engaged in building a renewed society, under the patronage of Our Lady.

Mission

Our shared commitments

  1. Forming priests, religious, and lay faithful in continuing discipleship.
  2. Strengthening small Christian communities grounded in the Word and the Church's teaching.
  3. Establishing programs that serve the diocese, vicariates, and parishes alike.
  4. Responding with Christian compassion to changes facing families and society.
